Literary Devices Comparison

Main connection: Suspense (a literary effect).

The Most Dangerous Game — Foreshadowing
Evidence: “The place has a reputation—a bad one.”
Explanation: Whitney's warning about Ship-Trap Island hints at the danger Rainsford will encounter. The warning builds suspense before the hunt begins.
Page number: Depends on the textbook edition; near the beginning.

The Cask of Amontillado — Symbolism
Evidence: “The man wore motley.”
Explanation: Fortunato's jester costume symbolizes his foolishness and failure to recognize Montresor's deception. It contributes to the suspense because the reader knows Fortunato is in danger.
Page number: Depends on the textbook edition; near the beginning.

Connection: Both stories build suspense around characters facing danger. Connell uses foreshadowing to hint at the threat ahead, while Poe uses symbolism to highlight Fortunato's vulnerability.
